🔒 Privacy-First Architecture

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Our Client-Side Architecture Guarantee

FinCast was engineered specifically so that your sensitive personal financial numbers (such as account balances, income, debt amounts, and interest rates) never leave your device. All calculations and charts are computed in real-time in your browser’s local memory using Vanilla JavaScript.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT ask for your name, Social Security number, or date of birth.
  • We do NOT connect to your bank accounts, Plaid, or credit reporting bureaus.
  • We do NOT store your simulator inputs in remote databases.

3. Local Storage

To provide a seamless experience if you refresh or return to a calculator, temporary simulation state may be saved to your browser's local localStorage. This data resides strictly on your machine and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

4. Analytics and Data Sharing

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to measure page views and general site usage. Google may process technical information such as browser, device, and interaction data under its own privacy terms. FinCast's custom analytics events include only tool and interaction labels; calculator amounts, balances, income, and other financial inputs are not included. See Google's Privacy Policy for details about Google's data processing.

5. Third-Party Advertising & Cookies

In future updates, we may use standard advertising partners (e.g., Google AdSense) or vetted affiliate partners. These partners may use cookies to serve relevant advertisements. You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ad Settings or the Digital Advertising Alliance.
