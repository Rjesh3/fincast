How to Crush Debt Fast: Avalanche vs. Snowball Explained

The Debt Avalanche (The Mathematical Winner)

The Debt Avalanche method organizes debts by interest rate from highest to lowest. You make minimum payments on all accounts and channel every single extra dollar toward the highest-APR balance (usually credit cards charging 20–29% APR).

Best for: Analytical individuals who want to minimize total interest paid to banks and become debt-free in the shortest mathematical time frame.

The Debt Snowball (The Psychological Winner)

The Debt Snowball method lists debts by total balance from smallest to largest, ignoring interest rates. By knocking out the smallest account first, you get an immediate psychological victory and roll that minimum payment into the next account.

Best for: Individuals who benefit from quick motivational milestones to stay disciplined throughout a multi-year debt payoff journey.
