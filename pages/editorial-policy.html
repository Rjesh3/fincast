Editorial Policy & Standards

How we research, write, verify calculations, update financial figures, and ensure uncompromised mathematical integrity.

1. Rigorous Formula & Math Verification

Every calculator engine on FinCast must be verified against independent institutional standards (e.g., SEC compounding calculators, IRS standard deduction worksheets, and Federal Reserve credit rate releases) prior to publication.

2. Transparent Assumptions

We never present financial estimates without stating the underlying mathematical assumptions (e.g. constant annualized return rates, discrete monthly compounding periods, or 2.8% baseline inflation discounting).

3. AI Transparency Policy

While we leverage modern computational tools and AI assistance for data structuring and research drafting, all content, calculations, and mathematical models undergo strict human review and factual verification. We never publish unverified automated AI content.

4. Correction & Update Cadence

Whenever tax laws or interest rate benchmarks change (such as annual IRS standard deduction adjustments or Social Security wage base changes), our data models are updated promptly. If a user identifies a calculation bug, we investigate and resolve it within 48 business hours.
