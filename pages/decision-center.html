FinCast does not give generic dogmatic advice. We model the mathematical trade-offs of your choices so you can make informed decisions.

High Intent

Should I Pay Off Debt or Invest?

If your debt interest is above 7–8% (credit cards, personal loans), paying debt delivers a guaranteed, risk-free return beating historical market averages. If your debt is low-rate fixed mortgage (<4%), investing in index funds mathematically produces higher wealth.

Mortgage Choice

15-Year vs. 30-Year Mortgage?

A 15-year loan cuts total interest by 60%, but locks you into a significantly higher mandatory monthly payment. A 30-year loan provides cash flow flexibility with the option to prepay principal whenever you choose.

Tax Strategy

Roth IRA vs. Traditional 401(k)?

Pay taxes now (Roth) if you are early in your career in lower tax brackets. Take upfront tax deductions now (Traditional) if you are currently in your peak earning years.

Safety Net

3 Months vs. 6 Months Emergency Fund?

Dual-income stable W2 households can safely maintain a 3-month buffer. Single earners, commission workers, or business owners should target 6–9 months.
