Understanding the Mechanics of Compound Interest

Why Starting 5 Years Earlier Changes Everything

Compound interest is exponential, not linear. In the first decade, your total balance is primarily fueled by your out-of-pocket deposits. In years 15–30, the annual returns generated by your investment gains far exceed your annual contributions.

The Power of the 3% Annual Contribution Step-Up

By committing to increase your monthly contribution by just 3% every year (roughly matching typical annual wage increases or cost-of-living adjustments), you dramatically accelerate your final net worth without feeling a sudden pinch in your lifestyle.
