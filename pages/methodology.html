Calculation Methodology & Assumptions

At FinCast, we believe financial tools should never be a black box. Below is the full mathematical foundation, formulas, and institutional sources powering our decision simulators.

1. Debt Payoff & Amortization Mechanics

All debt amortization calculations calculate monthly interest accrual using the standard nominal APR formula:

Monthly Interest Rate (r) = (Annual APR / 100) / 12
Monthly Interest Accrued = Current Balance × r
Principal Reduction = Total Monthly Payment - Monthly Interest Accrued

Infinite Payoff Safeguard: If scheduled payments are less than or equal to the monthly interest accrued, the simulator alerts the user that the debt balance will never decrease.

Avalanche vs Snowball Algorithmic Sorting: In the Debt Avalanche strategy, open debts are sorted by APR in descending order (highest rate targeted first). In the Debt Snowball strategy, open debts are sorted by balance in ascending order (smallest balance targeted first). In both strategies, when an account reaches zero, 100% of its minimum payment rolls over into the next target debt.

2. Compound Interest & Wealth Accumulation

Future value projections with monthly recurring deposits use the discrete compound interest formula with periodic compounding intervals:

FV = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t) + PMT × [ ((1 + r/n)^(n×t) - 1) / (r/n) ]

Where P is starting principal, r is annual return rate, n is compounding frequency (12 = monthly), t is time in years, and PMT is regular periodic contribution.

Inflation Discounting: When the inflation adjustment toggle is active, future purchasing power is discounted using the standard real-terms formula: Real Value = Nominal FV / (1 + inflation_rate)^t using the historical US baseline inflation rate of 2.8%.

3. Financial Health Score Scoring Weights

The 0–100 Financial Health Score is a composite weighted evaluation across 6 fundamental personal finance pillars:

  • • Emergency Fund Coverage (20% weight): Evaluates months of non-discretionary expenses held in liquid cash (Target: 3–6 months).
  • • Debt-to-Income / DTI (20% weight): Evaluates non-mortgage debt service burden against gross monthly income (Target: <15%).
  • • Cash Flow Surplus Margin (15% weight): Measures monthly income margin remaining after all living expenses and minimum debt payments.
  • • Savings & Investing Rate (15% weight): Evaluates percentage of monthly income routed to retirement accounts and long-term investments (Target: 15–20%+).
  • • Age-Based Retirement Trajectory (15% weight): Compares total invested assets against established age-based income multiples (e.g. 1x salary at 30, 3x at 40, 6x at 50).
  • • Revolving Credit Utilization (15% weight): Measures credit card balance against available credit lines (Target: <10–30%).

4. Authoritative Benchmark Sources

Our benchmarks and heuristics are informed by established institutional research and regulatory guidelines:

  • Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB): Debt-to-income and credit utilization thresholds.
  • Federal Reserve Consumer Credit Data (G.19): Average revolving credit APR trends.
  • U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC): Compound interest mechanics and investor education standards.
  • Internal Revenue Service (IRS): Annual retirement contribution ceilings and standard deduction references.
